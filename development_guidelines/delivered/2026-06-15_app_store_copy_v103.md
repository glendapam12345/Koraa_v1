### Description
```
If your day shifts with how you feel, rigid to-do lists don’t help. Koraa is emotional wellness with gentle task support: first you check in, then the app suggests a few steps — not everything you “should” do.

In seconds you log emotion, energy, time, and mental clarity. Koraa uses that to suggest what might matter today: 2–5 steps, no pressure. The rest can wait.

• Tasks: brain-dump in one place; optional projects when they help.
• Today: check-in + suggested steps at your pace.
• Calendar: your week, calmly (free plan: partial view).
• For Me: mood and energy patterns after a few check-ins.
• Tips: gentle ideas aligned to how you feel.

Koraa doesn’t shout or demand performance. Clear UI, space, and calm.

Koraa Premium (optional) adds full calendar, charts and insights in For Me, the full tip library, and more depth. 7-day free trial on subscription; cancel anytime in Apple Settings.

Privacy and account: you’re in control. Sign out and delete your account from the app.
```
